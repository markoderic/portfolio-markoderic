Classic Cat Mario / Syobon Action

Included in Marko Deric’s portfolio. Original game by Chiku; later stages by Bluvel (大川和樹). Open Syobon port by Mathew Velasquez. Web port and supplied replacement music by Takashi Toyoshima.

This is Toyoshima’s classic 0.9.2.0 port, revision 51691407c93637c95a70c16437e146d78e30de63. Artwork is the nine unchanged PNGs from that package. The app icon uses its player sprite. No original soundtrack, additional levels or replacement game was added.

Incomplete port audio: dungeon, star4, castle, puyo, goal, 4-clear and allclear have no supplied scores. Missing music stops the prior track; missing effects are silent. Audio output also depends on browser support and the portfolio’s sound preference.
